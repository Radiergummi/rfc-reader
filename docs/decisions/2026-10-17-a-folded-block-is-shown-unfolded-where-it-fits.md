# A folded block is shown unfolded where it fits

*Recorded 17 October 2026 from ARCHITECTURE.md (#756); decided with #64 and #212.*

A block folded per RFC 8792 is shown unfolded, without its header, when every unfolded line fits the column at full size, and otherwise exactly as the page printed it, header and backslashes included (`DocumentTextBuilder.displayedText`, issue #64). A block that does not fit is not scaled down to fit unfolded, because its unfolded lines can be far longer than the widths fit-to-measure scaling was measured against. "Copy Figure" and a code block's copy button always copy it unfolded, as a column wide enough shows it: tabs expanded, and source code without the indent its lines share, so a selection over the whole block pastes the same (`FoldedLines` in RFCKit, `FigureCopy.pasteboardText` in RFCReaderKit). A plain-text copy of a selection over a block shown folded is unfolded too, joining each fold whose backslash and continuation are both selected and leaving out a selected header (`FoldedLines.unfold(selection:strategy:)` from `SelectionText`, #212). Whether a block is shown folded is the build's to say (`VerbatimBox.shownFolding`): one shown unfolded is copied as shown, since under the `'\\'` strategy unfolding it again could join two of the author's own lines. The rich flavors keep the folded lines.
